# The warden head's name, alias and old line

## Status

Accepted

## Context

The warden head (note 09, issue #177) was written as a quiet Aragorn-like leader
with no hidden crown (decided 2026-10-28). On 2026-11-09 the owner chose her
names and asked that she come from an old ruling line that matters to the
ending. That crosses the earlier no hidden crown rule, so it needs a decision.
ADR 0003 still holds: nothing may come from Tolkien's content.

## Decision

- Her real name is Aldreth, one name and no surname, heard at stage 4. Her
  ring 1 alias is Lane, one word and no title. Strider is Tolkien's alias for
  Aragorn and is not used.
- She descends from the old ruling line of the kingdom the dark lord broke. It
  is a real part of the story and it matters to the ending (ring 3, issue #5).
  How it matters is not yet designed.
- The limits stay. No hidden crown, no sword that is reforged, no claim to a
  throne, no heir who must take a seat to win, and no ranger's cloak. The line
  shapes the ending through what she and the player choose, not through a crown
  she inherits. The empty crown stays the dark lord's emblem (note 09), so a
  crown must not become hers.
- Everything else about her stays optional, and the cheerful tone and the
  no-pressure rule (note 09, Neglect) still apply.

## Consequences

- Note 09 records the names and the line, and the ending design must use the
  line within the limits above.
- Her look, voice and stage 5 errands keep to the decided shape. The old line
  can inform stage 4 and stage 5 talk, not new errands.
- The warden head is no longer a pure Qi-type figure. She has a part in the
  ending. This should be set against issue #5 and the Last Seat in note 09.
