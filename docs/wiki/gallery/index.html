    The library gallery

    Dunmar, the librarian, takes one copy of each find for the gallery in the library hall: 53 minerals and 42 artifacts, 95 in all. Donate from your pack at the library desk; spares stay yours. Donated pieces can be arranged anywhere on the shelf, and the shelf can be read from the Collections page.

    Donating every piece pays one of the seven elderfruit. Donating 40 and donating all earn a hat each. The rewards below are not final.
