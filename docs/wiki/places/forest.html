    The Forest

    Size 120 by 120 tiles
    Riding Ride a horse here
    Fishing 7 fish
    Buildings Forest farmstead, Hermit tower

    A big wood north of the farm, with its own lake, forage in every season and the old farmstead.

    Forage

    • Spring: Wild horseradish, Dandelion, Wild leek, Daffodil, Morel
    • Summer: Sweet pea, Spice berry, Wild grape, Wild ginger
    • Fall: Field mushroom, Bramble berry, Crab apple, Wild garlic, Chanterelle
    • Winter: Crocus, Winter cress, Snowdrop, Holly sprig, Snowcap mushroom
