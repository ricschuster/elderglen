    The Catch

    Room The Last Seat
    Needs All 4
    Reward Magnet ring
    Hint The finest of each water: a stream, a lake, a sea and a night fish, all gold quality.

    What the hall accepts

    • A stream fish: any of Reedling, Brookslip, Millrun, Salmon, Hearthside Dace
    • A lake fish: any of Carp, Fernfin, Cold Quill, Marshlight
    • A sea fish: any of Sardine, Skiff Sprat, Mullet, Cod, Kelp Ribbon, Mackerel
    • A night fish: any of Moonmirror, Reed Cat, Herring, Lantern Squid
