# 114. Rosmarin's odd requests (note 113 open item)

Drafted and settled with the owner on 2026-11-25. Feeds the fairy ring prices (notes 09 and 113).
Stardew claims are from the saved Special Orders page (read 2026-11-25) unless marked otherwise. The Qi
shop stock is from fan sources (Destructoid, Holdtoreset, TheGamer, Game Rant; searched 2026-11-25),
because the saved pages do not list it.

## Stardew reference

- Qi's board sits in Qi's Walnut Room on Ginger Island, opened by 100 Golden Walnuts, so it is late game
  (after the Community Center, the boat and the island). Saved Special Orders page.
- It refreshes every Monday with two orders to choose from. Orders repeat over time. The time limit is 7
  to 28 days by order and counts down from Monday whether or not the order is accepted. Saved page.
- Orders pay Qi Gems, spent at Qi's shop in the same room. Golden Walnuts later trade 1 for 2 gems (fan
  sources).
- Qi's shop (fan sources, prices vary by source and version): Junimo Chest about 15, Horse Flute 50,
  Missing Stocklist 50, Hopper 10, Enricher 20, Pressure Nozzle 20, Deconstructor 20, Key to the Town 20,
  Galaxy Soul 40, Hyper Speed-Gro recipe 30, Deluxe Fertilizer recipe 20, Magic Bait 5, Qi Seasoning 10,
  Mushroom Tree Seed 5, Golden Egg 100 (after Perfection). A broad late-game convenience shop.

## Decided (owner, 2026-11-25)

1. **A token currency, as Qi Gems are (Stardew).** Rosmarin's odd requests pay a currency of her own
   (working name "odd marks", placeholder), spent at her shelf. The "one item from her odd requests" in
   each ring price (notes 09 and 113) becomes a part bought with odd marks at her shelf.
2. **A weekly board, as Qi's (Stardew).** A note on her door each Monday with two requests to choose
   from, one active at a time, a time limit of 7 to 28 days by request that counts from Monday whether
   accepted or not, and requests repeat over time.
3. **Opens on ring 2 arrival (Stardew-like timing).** The board opens when the player first reaches
   ring 2 (M5), the same place in the game as Qi's room after the island. So the system is built with
   M5, not M3c.
4. **Her shelf is like Qi's shop (Stardew).** A broad late-game shop: ring parts, rare seeds,
   convenience machines and tools, recipes and a few odd furnishings. The stock list is drafted below
   for the owner to check.

## Draft (placeholders, owner to check)

- **Requests:** harder versions of the town's special orders (note 112 rule 4) with an odd twist in her
  voice: "a fish caught in the rain", "stones from floor 100 down", "a crop grown on a single day of
  rain". About 12 to start, each with its own time limit and 5 to 20 odd marks.
- **Shelf stock, mapped from Qi's shop to things we have or plan:** each fairy ring's part (one per
  ring), a pond enricher, a pressure nozzle for sprinklers, a hopper, magic bait (x20), a mushroom tree
  seed, a speed-gro recipe, a larger double bed, and a house key style item for the town. Prices in odd
  marks at tuning (note 07).

## Ring prices (owner, 2026-11-25)

- **Ring 1 rings cost gold and items only (Stardew, saved Wizard's Tower page: obelisks take gold and
  items, no Qi Gems).** The mountain and beach rings (note 113) drop the odd-request part and can be built
  in ring 1. The ring 2 and ring 3 rings still take one part from her shelf.
