# 107: Audio (sound effects, ambience, music)

Status: designed and built 2026-11-21 (branch `feat/audio`); built to first-pass level, not yet auditioned by ear. Closes the design question in #527 (the
issue has no other content: music approach, effects list, mix and settings, licensing). Decision record:
`docs/decisions/0017-audio.md`. Placeholder numbers throughout; every level is a tuning knob.

Goal: about 60 percent of how a cozy farming game sounds and feels: feedback on every action, a music
bed that follows season, place, time and weather, and a quiet ambience under it. The game was silent
before this note. Stardew is a black box (ADR 0002): no audio is copied or imitated note for note.
Every effect and every synthesised track is original, and every downloaded track is CC0 or CC-BY with a
row in `docs/audio/LICENCES.md`.

## Where sound comes from

| Kind | Source | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Effects and ambient loops | Synthesised by `scripts/audio/` (seeded, deterministic), rendered offline to Ogg Vorbis | Fully original, reproducible, small, no runtime CPU or garbage on tablets, same file path as a bought or downloaded sound |
| Music | Downloaded CC0 tracks where one fits, otherwise a synthesised placeholder loop composed as data in `scripts/audio/music/` | Owner preference; every slot always plays something |

Every slot is swappable: drop a file at `public/audio/<kind>/<id>.ogg` and list the id in
`scripts/audio/external.json` (or run `npm run audio:ingest`). The build then stops rendering that id.

## Files, formats, budget

- Served from `public/audio/{sfx,ambient,music}/`. Ogg Vorbis everywhere (Chromium WebView on Android,
  Electron and desktop browsers all decode it). No mp3 fallback needed. iOS is not a target (ADR 0008).
- Sample rate 44.1 kHz. Effects and ambience mono, synthesised music mono, downloaded music stereo.
- Budget: under 30 MB shipped (the verifier fails over 28 MB). Target about 20 MB.
- `src/content/audio-manifest.json` is generated by `npm run audio:build` (ids, files, levels).
  `src/content/audio-rules.json` is hand kept (map to area table, ducking, aliases, defaults).

## Mixer and settings

Buses: `master` -> `music`, `sfx`, `ambient`. Settings (saved with the other device settings in
`Settings`, `src/core/bindings.ts`): `musicVolume`, `sfxVolume`, `ambientVolume` (0 to 100, steps of 10
in Options) and `muted`. Defaults: music 60, effects 80, ambient 60. A slider maps to gain by a square
curve (`(v/100)^2`) so the low end is usable. Music tracks are normalised to about -18 LUFS, ambient
loops to about -28 LUFS, effects by peak (default -6 dBFS, per sound `peakDb`) in the build; the mixer
adds per-id `gain` from the manifest on top.

Ducking: music drops by 40 percent (`duckMusic` in the rules) for 1.6 s under a stinger (level up,
bundle complete, heart event start, gift loved, marriage). Music changes cross-fade over 2.5 s
(`crossfadeMs`); a stinger or event track cross-fades faster (0.8 s).

Autoplay: the context is created on the first pointer or key press and resumed then. It is suspended
when the tab or app is hidden (`visibilitychange`, and Capacitor/Electron pause the page the same way)
and resumed on return. Nothing throws if audio is unavailable (tests, old WebViews): the engine is a
no-op.

## Variation

Each sound def has `variants` (files rendered with different seeds), `pitch` (playback rate jitter,
plus or minus), `gainJitter` and `cooldownMs`. The picker never plays the same variant twice in a row.
Footsteps use 3 variants per surface, hits and tool strikes 3, animal cues 2.

## Hook points

One central director (`src/audio/director.ts`) reads the running scenes and a probe the farm scene
publishes, so music, ambience and menu open or close sounds need no scattered calls. Direct calls exist
only where an action has a precise moment: tools and their outcomes in `FarmScene.use` and `report`,
footsteps in `FarmScene.update`, doors and travel, harvest and pick up, combat in `farm-combat.ts`,
fishing in `FishingScene`, selling in `ShippingScene` and `ShopScene`, bundles in `HallScene`, heart
events (the event data's `music` and `sfx` commands), the wedding, the end of the day, festivals.
The mapping from a use outcome to a sound, the footstep surface and all selection rules are pure code
in `src/core/audio.ts` with unit tests.

Every defined id is now hooked. Menu tabs play `ui.tab`, switches in Options `ui.toggle`, a new HUD line
`ui.notify` (skipped when an action already sounded). The director adds `clock.chime` (noon, dusk, midnight),
`machine.done` and `item.drop` from changes in the world. The morning report plays `sprinkler.run`, the
animals' calls, `mail` and `achievement`. Felling a tree, ore and geodes, loading a machine, crafting, closing
a chest, and the festival parade or hunt ending have their own cues. Animals call by species when fed or petted.
Limits: an id never retriggers faster than 30 ms, has at most 4 voices at once, and all effects at most 24.
Animals other than the coop and barn cues, and sounds for other players in a shared valley, are not
hooked either.

## Music slots and selection

Slots (ids): `title`, `spring`, `summer`, `fall`, `winter` (farm by day), `night`, `rain`, `village`,
`mines`, `beach`, `woods`, `interior`, `festival`, `night-market`, `heart-event`, `wedding`, `bundle`
(a stinger, no loop), `fishing`. Twelve slots use downloaded CC0 tracks (title, spring, summer, fall, winter, night, rain, village, woods,
beach, interior, and the bundle stinger); six are synthesised placeholders (mines, festival, night-market,
heart-event, wedding, fishing). Which is which is in
`docs/audio/LICENCES.md` and `scripts/audio/external.json`.

Priority, first match wins (`chooseMusic`):

1. Scene overrides: title, options, lobby and farm choice play `title`; heart event scene plays
   `heart-event` (or the event data's `music` id, mapped through the aliases); wedding scene `wedding`;
   the end scene and a completed hall `bundle`; the fishing scene `fishing`.
2. Festival: on a festival map on a festival day `festival`; the Night Market after its opening time
   (and outdoors in the village) `night-market`.
3. Place: a mine floor `mines`; maps of class `beach` (cove, delta) `beach`; class `woods` (forest,
   secret woods, forest farmstead) `woods`; class `village` (green, hearthwell, hall, outpost) `village`;
   class `interior` (shops, homes, barn, coop, inn) `interior`.
4. Farm and open ground: night (after nightfall for the season) `night`; raining or stormy `rain`;
   otherwise the season track. Snow plays `winter`.

The map to class table is `areas` in `audio-rules.json`; unknown maps are `farm`. A tab or menu opened
over the farm does not change the music (the farm scene is paused, not gone).

Ambience (`chooseAmbient`, several layers at once, each with a level 0 to 1): birds outdoors by day
(not in rain or winter); crickets outdoors at night (not winter, not rain); rain when raining (quieter
indoors); wind in winter, in storms and on the mountain; waves on beach class maps; drip in mines and
the cellar; hearth in interiors. Layers cross-fade over 1.5 s.

## Verification

- `npm run audio:verify`: per file, clipping, true peak, integrated loudness, head and tail silence,
  loop seam (jump between last and first sample against the local slope), duration, and total size.
- Unit tests: ids used in code and in the aliases exist in the manifest; every manifest file exists;
  music selection, ambient selection, variant picking, mixer maths, settings round trip.
- `npm run playtest:audio`: drives real play and asserts audio events fired, the music track changed,
  and the console stayed clean.
- Audition page: `audio.html` (dev only) lists every sound and track with play buttons.

## Tuning knobs

Per sound: `scripts/audio/sfx/*.mjs` (`gain`, `peakDb`, `pitch`, `gainJitter`, `variants`, `cooldownMs`)
and the recipe beside it. Per track: `scripts/audio/music/tracks.mjs`. Mixer defaults, ducking, cross
fade times, map classes: `src/content/audio-rules.json`. Add the numbers to
`docs/design/07_tuning_numbers.md` only when they are balanced against something; they are not.

## Out of scope

Voice acting, spatial (panned) sound, per-villager themes, dynamic stems, and Stardew-like exact
mappings. A later pass can add panning by distance and more stingers.
