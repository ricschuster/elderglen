## Completed

- Playtest scripts (`scripts/playtest/play.mjs`, `lib.mjs`). The Hearthwell failures from 2026-11-14c were not
  script rot in the doors. Causes found and fixed:
  - The general store opens at 9:00 and is closed one day a week (`src/content/shops.json`, Wed). The bot
    arrived at about 6:30. It now waits for the clock before it leaves the farm, and skips the shop (no
    finding) when the door is shut. The door message is now in the finding text.
  - A new farm starts with trees, a rock and other blockers on the plot rows, so the fixed plot cells were
    often unreachable. `mapFreeCells` moves each template cell to the nth free tile on the same row, and
    skips exit tiles (the greenhouse ruin at 26,13 cannot be tilled).
  - Store interior tiles were stale: exit stand 9,28 (door 9,29), counter stand 6,19 (counter 6,18).
  - `hookWalk` timed out after a flat 20 s. Long walks take longer on a busy machine, so it now gives up
    after 8 s with no tile change, or 2 minutes in all.
  - Forage walled in by trees is skipped with a log line, not a finding. Morning story screens (orders
    intro, Greencoat) are dismissed before the "report did not close" check.
- Phone: Capacitor's `SystemBars` plugin padded the WebView by the cutout inset, a black strip on a Pixel 9 Pro.
  `capacitor.config.ts` now sets `insetsHandling: "disable"`, and `MainActivity` draws into the cutout on
  Android 9 and later. The owner confirmed the strip is gone. Not checked: whether any on-screen control sits
  under the camera hole.
