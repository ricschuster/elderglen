# Audio licence ledger

Every third party audio file shipped in `public/audio/` has a row here: file, title, author, licence,
source URL. Licence bar: CC0 or CC-BY. A CC-BY file also needs an entry in the Options credits screen
(`src/content/audio-credits.json`). Files not listed here are our own: the effects, ambient loops and
placeholder music rendered by `scripts/audio/` (original, no third party material, no Stardew audio;
ADR 0002 and ADR 0017).

Downloaded tracks were chosen from page descriptions and measured loudness only, not auditioned by
ear (the build has no ears). Audition them with `audio.html` and swap any that do not fit.
